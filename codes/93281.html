Cardiac Electrophysiology CPT Codes

Cardiac Electrophysiology CPT codes include common office visits, EKG interpretation, cardioversion, external rhythm monitoring, pacemaker and ICD interrogation/programming, remote device monitoring, pacemaker/ICD/CRT procedures, loop recorder insertion, leadless pacemaker procedures, and EP ablation procedures. WRVU values shown are physician work RVUs and are intended for education, documentation awareness, and case-building support.
