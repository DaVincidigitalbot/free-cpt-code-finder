Coding Pearl

In many hospitals, a general surgeon, trauma surgeon, acute care surgeon, or vascular surgeon performs the anterior exposure for ALIF procedures and assists throughout the operation.

When appropriate and permitted by payer policy, modifier 80 is commonly appended to both CPT 22558 and each reported CPT 22585 level when the assistant surgeon participated in all operative levels.

Do not confuse modifier 80 (Assistant Surgeon) with modifier 62 (Co-Surgeons).

Documentation and Coding Notes

AdSense readiness coding note: CPT 22585 should be treated as an educational starting point, not a final billing instruction. The operative note should clearly support the approach, anatomic level, clinical indication, and each separately reportable service.

Before submitting a claim or logging the case, compare CPT 22585 with adjacent codes in the same family, confirm current AMA CPT language, check CMS/NCCI edits, and verify payer-specific assistant-surgeon and co-surgeon rules.
