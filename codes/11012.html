When to Use CPT 11012

Use CPT 11012 for debridement associated with an open fracture or dislocation when the documented excisional debridement includes skin, subcutaneous tissue, muscle fascia, muscle, and bone.

Documentation and Modifier Notes

  • Document that debridement was surgical/excisional, including the instrument used and tissue actually removed.
  • Document the open fracture or dislocation context and the deepest layer debrided.
  • Do not bill active wound care codes 97597/97598 for the same wound on the same date as excisional debridement.
  • Modifier guidance: See payer/NCCI guidance; do not bill 97597/97598 for the same wound on the same date.
