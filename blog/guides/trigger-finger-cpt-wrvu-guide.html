Documentation Notes

  • Document laterality when applicable and support LT/RT or bilateral reporting with the operative note.
  • For fracture work, document open versus closed treatment, fixation method, number of fragments when relevant, and associated dislocation or tendon/nerve work.
  • For tendon and nerve procedures, document named structure, zone/location, repair versus transfer/decompression, and whether separate codes are supported by NCCI and payer policy.
